## Constraint breakdown

**Height and an 8-foot ceiling.** Both racks are shorter than 96 inches, and both have an unpublished minimum ceiling. The useful number is leftover clearance: 3.7 inches for the REP, 5.625 inches for the Rogue. The Rogue is 1.925 inches shorter. If pull-ups under an 8-foot ceiling matter, that 1.925-inch gap is the clearest published difference. Neither manufacturer publishes pull-up bar height on these SKUs.

**Width and footprint.** The REP takes 48 inches of wall and 7.17 sq ft of floor. The Rogue takes 56.1875 inches of wall and 9.66 sq ft. That is 8.1875 inches of extra wall width and 2.49 sq ft of extra floor. Inside the uprights, the Rogue is slightly wider (43 in vs the REP's 42.1 in inside width).

**Open depth.** The 21.5-inch label does not mean the same thing on both racks. On the REP, open depth is 21.5 inches. On the Rogue, 21.5 inches is inside depth; depth from the wall is 24.75 inches. If the rack has to live along a tight walkway, the Rogue needs 3.25 inches more than the REP when open.

**Folded depth and fold direction.** Both fold inward. The Rogue's 21.5-inch version can also fold outward, which is useful if a door, radiator, or other wall obstacle sits inside the fold path. The REP's folded depth is a published 6 inches. The Rogue's folded depth is Unverified; the manufacturer only states "less than 5 inches."

**Uprights, holes, and capacity.** Both are 3x3, 11-gauge. The REP publishes 0.625-inch holes and 1-inch spacing through the bench and pull area. The Rogue publishes Westside spacing in the bench/pull area and 2-inch spacing above and below; hole size is Unverified, though the hardware is 5/8 inch. Capacity is 1,000 lb on the REP when properly mounted, and Not published on the Rogue.

**Mounting and stringers.** A wood stringer of two 2x10x56-inch boards is not included with the REP, and the REP Stringer is not compatible. Concrete mounting uses included M10x90 expansion bolts. Rogue's optional stringer pair is sold separately, highly recommended, and requires 4 wood studs. If you plan to use spotter arms on the Rogue, the manufacturer says the wall attachment should be checked by a contractor. Stud spacing and floor-bolt requirements are Unverified on both.

**Included hardware.** Both include J-cups and a pull-up bar. Neither includes safeties. The REP pull-up bar is specified as 1.25 inches; Rogue's bar diameter is not published here.

## Buy the REP PR-4100 if

- Your wall is closer to 48 inches than 56 inches, or you want the smaller 7.17 sq ft footprint.
- You need a published 21.5-inch open depth and a published 6-inch folded depth.
- You want a published 1,000 lb capacity and 0.625-inch holes with 1-inch spacing in the bench and pull area.
- You are mounting to concrete and can use the included M10x90 expansion bolts, or you can supply the two 2x10x56-inch boards yourself.
- You can live with 3.7 inches of clearance under a 96-inch ceiling.

## Buy the Rogue RML-3W if

- You want the extra 1.925 inches of overhead clearance (5.625 in vs 3.7 in) under an 8-foot ceiling.
- You need the 21.5-inch version's ability to fold inward or outward.
- You want the slightly wider 43-inch inside-upright dimension and will accept 56.1875 inches on the wall and 24.75 inches of depth from the wall.
- You are prepared to add the recommended 4-stud stringer, which is sold separately.
- You can accept an unverified folded depth, unverified hole size, and unpublished weight capacity.
