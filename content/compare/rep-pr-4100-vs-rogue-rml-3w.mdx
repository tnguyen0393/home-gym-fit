## Quick verdict

The [REP PR-4100](/products/rep-pr-4100-folding-wall-mount-rack-21-5) and the [Rogue RML-3W](/products/rogue-rml-3w-fold-back-wall-mount-rack-21-5) are both 3x3, 11-gauge, inward-folding wall-mount racks.

They are not the same size on the wall, and they do not leave the same room under an 8-foot ceiling.

- **Pick the REP PR-4100 if** you need the smaller wall width and footprint, plus published 21.5 in open and 6 in folded depths. Clearance under 96 in is tighter.
- **Pick the Rogue RML-3W if** you want more overhead under 96 in and an inward or outward fold. You give up wall width, open depth, and a published folded depth.

Both require wall mounting, and neither includes safeties. See the four-rack view in [folding power racks under an 8-foot ceiling](/guides/folding-power-racks-8-foot-ceiling/), or [browse the directory](/directory/).

- **Full model names:** PR-4100 Folding Squat Rack (21.5" Depth); RML-3W Fold Back Wall Mount Rack (21.5" Inside Depth).
- **REP width:** Inside width 42.1 in; inside length 18.9 in.
- **Rogue width and depth:** 56.1875 in is overall on-wall width. Open depth is 24.75 in from the wall, with 21.5 in inside.
- **Rogue folded depth:** The manufacturer states only "less than 5 in." The 21.5 in version can also fold outward.
- **Rogue weight:** 163 lb is the 21.5 in option from a published "163LB or 190LB" pair.
- **REP holes and capacity:** 0.625 in (5/8 in); 1 in through the bench press and pull area, 2 in elsewhere. Capacity is 1,000 lb when properly mounted.
- **Rogue holes:** Westside through the bench/pull area; 2 in on center above and below. Hardware is 5/8 in; hole size is not published.
- **REP figures used here:** 92.3 in height, 48 in width, and 6 in folded depth. A legacy description lists 92 in, 48.5 in, and 6.5 in and is not used here.
- **Mounting:** A wood stringer of two 2x10x56 in boards is not included with the REP, and the REP Stringer is not compatible. Concrete mounting uses included M10x90 expansion bolts.
- **Rogue stringer:** The optional stringer pair is sold separately, highly recommended, and requires 4 wood studs. If you use spotter arms, Rogue says a contractor should check the wall attachment.

Manufacturer pages: [REP PR-4100](https://repfitness.com/products/pr-4100-folding-wall-mount-squat-power-rack) and [Rogue RML-3W](https://www.roguefitness.com/rogue-rml-3w-fold-back-wall-mount-rack).

The 21.5 in label does not mean the same thing on both racks. Along a tight walkway, the Rogue needs 3.25 in more than the REP when open.
