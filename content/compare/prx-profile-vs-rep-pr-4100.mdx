## Quick verdict

The [PRx Profile PRO](/products/prx-profile-pro-folding-squat-rack-89) folds up into the ceiling space. The [REP PR-4100](/products/rep-pr-4100-folding-wall-mount-rack-21-5) folds in toward the wall and leaves ceiling height alone.

Under an 8-foot (96-inch) ceiling, that is the whole comparison.

- **Pick the PRx Profile PRO if** you want a 4 in upward-fold profile, hydraulic-shock assist, and included wood-stud hardware at 16 or 24 in centers.
- **PRx tradeoff:** You must accept a modified install under 8 ft, and open depth at 96 in is not published.
- **Pick the REP PR-4100 if** you cannot spend ceiling space on an upward fold and want a published 6 in inward fold, a fixed 21.5 in open depth, and the smaller footprint.

Both are 3x3, 11-gauge wall-mount racks with 1,000 lb capacity, included J-cups and a pull-up bar, and no safeties.

- **Full model names:** Profile PRO Folding Squat Rack (89" Uprights, Black, Straight Pull-Up Bar); PR-4100 Folding Squat Rack (21.5" Depth).
- **PRx ceiling:** 94 in is the modified-install minimum; 107 in is recommended for a standard install.
- **PRx 7 in figure:** That is space above the 89 in uprights when the rack is down, not fold-up clearance. Folded-up height is not published.
- **PRx vs 96 in:** A 96 in room is 2 in above the 94 in modified minimum and 11 in short of the 107 in standard recommendation.
- **PRx size:** 53 in total width; 48 in outside of uprights. The 8.01 sq ft footprint is for the standard 107 in install.
- **REP width:** Inside width 42.1 in; inside length 18.9 in.
- **PRx open depth:** 21.75 in at a standard 107 in install; 16 in at a 94 in modified install; not published at 96 in. Do not interpolate a depth.
- **PRx mounting:** Wall brackets mount to 16 in or 24 in on-center studs, and a ledger board is required if the studs are unevenly spaced. All wood-stud brackets and hardware are included.
- **REP holes and capacity:** 0.625 in (5/8 in); 1 in through the bench press and pull area, 2 in elsewhere. Capacity is 1,000 lb when properly mounted.
- **PRx holes:** Front 2 in (1 in in the bench area); side 2 in. This SKU (PXP108) is the 89 in, black, straight-bar configuration.
- **REP mounting:** A wood stringer of two 2x10x56 in boards is not included, and the REP Stringer is not compatible. Concrete mounting uses included M10x90 expansion bolts.
- **REP figures used here:** 92.3 in height, 48 in width, and 6 in folded depth. A legacy description lists 92 in, 48.5 in, and 6.5 in and is not used here.
