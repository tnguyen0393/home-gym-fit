The decision between the [PRx Profile PRO](/products/prx-profile-pro-folding-squat-rack-89) and the [REP PR-4100](/products/rep-pr-4100-folding-wall-mount-rack-21-5) is not a close spec-sheet tie. ## Constraint breakdown

**Upward fold vs inward fold.** This is the fit question. The PRx stores by folding upward, with hydraulic-shock assist, to a 4-inch profile. That stored position spends ceiling space. The REP folds inward to a published 6-inch depth and does not take additional ceiling height when stored.

**Ceiling height math at 96 inches.** The PRx is the only rack of the two with a published minimum ceiling. A 96-inch room clears PRx's 94-inch modified-install minimum by 2 inches and falls 11 inches short of the 107-inch standard-install recommendation. When the PRx is down (in use), there are 7 inches between the 89-inch uprights and a 96-inch ceiling. That 7-inch figure is not fold-up clearance; PRx has not published a folded-up height. The REP has no published minimum ceiling. Subtracting verified height from 96 inches leaves 3.7 inches above the 92.3-inch REP.

**Open depth is not a fair 21.5 vs 21.75 comparison under 8-foot ceilings.** The REP's open depth is a fixed 21.5 inches regardless of ceiling height. The PRx's 21.75-inch depth is published for a standard install at a 107-inch ceiling. At a 94-inch modified install, PRx depth drops to 16 inches. PRx has not published a depth for a 96-inch ceiling. A 96-inch room sits 2 inches above the 94-inch point and 11 inches below the 107-inch point. Do not interpolate a depth between 16 and 21.75 inches.

**Width and footprint.** The REP is narrower and smaller on the floor: 48 inches wide and 7.17 sq ft. The PRx is 53 inches wide overall (48 inches outside the uprights) and 8.01 sq ft (standard 107 in install). Unit weight is Not published on both.

**Mounting and studs.** The PRx wall brackets mount to 16-inch or 24-inch on-center studs. A ledger board is required if the studs are unevenly spaced. All wall mounting brackets and hardware for wood-stud installation are included. The REP does not publish stud spacing. A wood stringer of two 2x10x56-inch boards is not included, and the REP Stringer is not compatible. Concrete mounting on the REP uses included M10x90 expansion bolts. Floor-bolt requirements are Unverified on both. Neither rack is renter-friendly.

**Included hardware.** Both are 3x3, 11-gauge, with 0.625-inch holes, 1,000 lb capacity, included J-cups, an included pull-up bar, and no safeties. Hole spacing is similar in spirit: both tighten to 1 inch in the bench area, with 2-inch spacing elsewhere (PRx also lists 2-inch side spacing). The REP pull-up bar is 1.25 inches; this PRx SKU (PXP108) is the 89-inch, black, straight-bar configuration.

## Buy the PRx Profile PRO if

- You want the 4-inch upward-fold profile and a hydraulic-shock assisted fold, and you have confirmed the ceiling can support that fold path.
- You are on wood studs at 16-inch or 24-inch centers (or you will add a ledger board) and want the wood-stud mounting brackets and hardware included.
- You can accept a modified install under an 8-foot ceiling: 2 inches above PRx's 94-inch modified minimum, 11 inches short of the 107-inch standard recommendation, with open depth at 96 inches unpublished (16 in at 94 in; 21.75 in at 107 in).
- A 53-inch overall width and 8.01 sq ft footprint (standard 107 in install) fit the wall.

## Buy the REP PR-4100 if

- You cannot spend ceiling space on an upward fold and want an inward fold that stores at a published 6 inches.
- You need a fixed 21.5-inch open depth that does not change with ceiling height.
- You want the narrower 48-inch width and the smaller 7.17 sq ft footprint.
- You are mounting to concrete and can use the included M10x90 expansion bolts, or you can supply the two 2x10x56-inch stringer boards (the REP Stringer will not work).
- You can live with only 3.7 inches of clearance above a 92.3-inch rack under a 96-inch ceiling.
