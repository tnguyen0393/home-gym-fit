## Quick verdict

All five benches here store upright. None of the manufacturers publish exact stored height, width, or depth.

The one published stored footprint is 3.1 sq ft, on the REP AB-5200 2.0. Do not interpolate stored size for the other four.

What you can still use for apartment fit is open length, bench weight, and the floor footprint when the bench is in use.

Open length runs from 47 in to 57.6 in. Bench weight runs from 69 lb to 125 lb, which you tip upright every session.

- **Lightest and shortest:** The [Ironmaster Super Bench PRO V2](/products/ironmaster-super-bench-pro-v2) is 69 lb and 47 in long. Overall width and stored footprint are not published.
- **Only published stored footprint:** The [REP AB-5200 2.0](/products/rep-ab-5200-2-0-adjustable-weight-bench) stores in 3.1 sq ft, but it is also the longest at 57.6 in and weighs 115 lb.
- **Lightest REP option:** The [REP Nighthawk](/products/rep-nighthawk-adjustable-weight-bench) is 88 lb and 51 in long, with a 700 lb rating. It succeeds the discontinued AB-4100.
- **Heaviest in this set:** The [Rogue Adjustable Bench 3.0](/products/rogue-adjustable-bench-3-0) weighs 125 lb, and weight capacity is not published. Buyable variants are labeled Adjustable Bench 3.1.
- **Flat bench, highest rating:** The [Titan Series Single Post](/products/titan-series-single-post-flat-bench) is flat only, 76 lb, and rated 1,200 lb, with a 14 in pad.

## Vertical storage reality check

Upright storage is confirmed on all five product pages. Exact stored height, width, and depth are not published for any of them.

Only the REP AB-5200 2.0 lists a stored footprint, at 3.1 sq ft. Treating the other four as the same size would be a guess.

Until those stored dimensions are published, size the room with the figures that are verified: open length, in-use footprint, and how heavy the bench is to tip.

Open length is the floor space the bench occupies during a session. It ranges from 47 in on the Ironmaster to 57.6 in on the AB-5200 2.0.

In-use footprints are published for the Nighthawk (9.2 sq ft) and the AB-5200 2.0 (10.33 sq ft). Rogue, Ironmaster, and Titan do not publish a square-foot footprint.

Tipping the bench upright every session is a lifting task. The Ironmaster is 69 lb with the incline seat; the Rogue is 125 lb.

Four of the five benches include transport wheels. Wheels are not published for the AB-5200 2.0.

The Nighthawk uses a rubber-coated upright storage post, the AB-5200 2.0 an integrated metal stand with a plastic liner, and the Rogue a built-in stand with rubber padding at the base.

## Fit verdicts

**[REP Nighthawk](/products/rep-nighthawk-adjustable-weight-bench)**
- **Best for:** An 88 lb adjustable bench with wheels, a 51 in open length, and a 9.2 sq ft footprint.
- **Watch out:** Capacity is 700 lb, and there is no decline. Stored dimensions are not published.
- [Manufacturer page](https://repfitness.com/products/rep-nighthawk-adjustable-bench)

**[REP AB-5200 2.0](/products/rep-ab-5200-2-0-adjustable-weight-bench)**
- **Best for:** The only published stored footprint, at 3.1 sq ft, plus a 1,000 lb rating and 10 back-pad positions.
- **Watch out:** It is the longest at 57.6 in and weighs 115 lb. Decline needs an optional post, and wheels are not published.
- [Manufacturer page](https://repfitness.com/products/ab-5200-2-0)

**[Rogue Adjustable Bench 3.0](/products/rogue-adjustable-bench-3-0)**
- **Best for:** A 24.75 in rear-foot width, 10 back-pad positions, and wheels plus a handle.
- **Watch out:** At 125 lb, it is the heaviest bench here. Weight capacity and stored dimensions are not published.
- **Also note:** Buyable variants are labeled Adjustable Bench 3.1. Pad height is 55.75 in at 85 degrees, not a stored height.
- [Manufacturer page](https://www.roguefitness.com/rogue-adjustable-bench-3-0)

**[Ironmaster Super Bench PRO V2](/products/ironmaster-super-bench-pro-v2)**
- **Best for:** The lightest (69 lb) and shortest (47 in) bench here, with 11 back-pad positions and wheels.
- **Watch out:** Overall width and stored footprint are not published. The incline and upright rating drops to 600 lb.
- [Manufacturer page](https://ironmaster.com/products/super-bench-pro-v2)

**[Titan Series Single Post](/products/titan-series-single-post-flat-bench)**
- **Best for:** A flat-only bench with a 1,200 lb rating, a 14 in by 4 in pad, 76 lb weight, and single-post foot clearance.
- **Watch out:** There are no incline, decline, or seat adjustments. Stored dimensions are not published.
- [Manufacturer page](https://titan.fitness/products/titan-series-single-post-flat-bench)
