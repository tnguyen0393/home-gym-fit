This guide covers every rack in the catalog marked `renter_friendly: true`. All five are freestanding: wall mounting is not required, and floor bolting is not required. That does not mean every one of them is stable without a bolt, and it does not mean all five fit a 7-foot ceiling.

## Fit verdict

If the room is under 84 inches (7 feet), only three of these five clear that height:

- [Titan T-2 Series Power Rack (71")](/products/titan-t-2-series-power-rack-71) — 71 in tall, 13 in of clearance under 84 in. The only full compact cage in this set that fits a 7-foot room.
- [Titan T-3 Series Short Squat Stand](/products/titan-t-3-series-short-squat-stand) — 73.5 in tall, 10.5 in of clearance. No pull-up bar and no safeties included.
- [REP Apollo Half Rack (80" Uprights)](/products/rep-apollo-half-rack-80) — 80 in tall, 4 in of clearance. Tight, and pull-up bar, J-cups, and safeties are Unverified because they depend on builder selections.

The other two are too tall for 84 inches:

- [REP PR-1100](/products/rep-pr-1100-power-rack) — 85 in, 1 in too tall for a 7-foot ceiling. It does fit under 96 inches, with 11 in of clearance.
- [Rogue HR-2 Half Rack (90" Front Uprights)](/products/rogue-hr-2-half-rack-90) — 92.25 in, 8.25 in too tall for 84 in, and only 3.75 in of clearance under 96 in.

Minimum ceiling is Not published on all five. The clearance figures are ceiling height minus verified rack height, not a manufacturer-stated minimum. The PR-1100's multi-grip pull-up bar can be inverted to reduce height, but the reduced height is not published, so this guide still treats the rack as 85 inches.

## Full cage vs half rack vs squat stand

**Compact power racks (full cages): REP PR-1100 and Titan T-2.** Both include J-cups, a pull-up bar, and pin-and-pipe safeties. Both use 2x2, 14-gauge uprights. The T-2 is the 7-foot-ceiling cage: 71 inches tall, 850 lb rackable capacity (2,200 lb whole-rack capacity), 2-inch hole spacing, 1-inch holes (the spec table also lists 26 mm; the details list says 1 in), 125 lb, with a 1.25-inch pull-up bar at a 69-inch max height, plus two weight-plate horns. The PR-1100 is the 8-foot-ceiling cage: 85 inches tall, 700 lb capacity, 3-inch hole spacing, 1-inch holes, 130 lb, 3/8-inch hardware. Muscle-ups are not recommended on the PR-1100, and banded movements are not recommended because there are no band-peg holes.

**Half racks: Rogue HR-2 and REP Apollo.** Both are 3x3, 11-gauge, and freestanding. The HR-2 includes a Monster Lite J-cup pair and a 43-inch single skinny pull-up bar. Safeties are not included; a 16-inch pin/pipe safety set is an optional add-on. Hole spacing is Westside; hole size and weight capacity are Not published. The base is 2x3-inch, 11-gauge. The Apollo has a published 1,000 lb rackable capacity, 1-inch holes, and 2-inch spacing. It uses a flat-foot base and 5000 Series attachments; it is not Ares or Belt Squat compatible. Pull-up bar, J-cups, and safeties on the Apollo are Unverified; they depend on builder selections.

**Squat stand: Titan T-3 Short Squat Stand.** This is the most stripped setup in the renter-friendly set. Uprights are 2x3, 11-gauge, with 0.6875-inch holes, Westside spacing through the bench and clean-pull zone, 2-inch spacing above and below, and 6-inch side hole spacing. Capacity is 1,000 lb. J-cups are included. The pull-up bar is not included, and safeties are not included; spotter arms are sold separately. Overall height is 73.5 inches on 72-inch uprights. Unit weight is 144 lb.

## Freestanding stability and no-bolting notes

All five have `wall_mount_required: false` and `floor_bolt_required: false`. The manufacturers still do not say the same thing about leaving them unbolted.

- **REP PR-1100.** The manufacturer states the PR-1100 cannot be bolted down. There is no bolt-down path if the rack shifts. [Manufacturer page](https://repfitness.com/products/pr-1100-power-rack).
- **Titan T-2 Power Rack.** Rubber feet with bolt-down options. Titan recommends bolting down for added stability. Floor bolting is not required, but the manufacturer's stability advice is to bolt it. That is the tension for renters who cannot drill the floor. [Manufacturer page](https://titan.fitness/products/t2-series-power-rack).
- **Rogue HR-2.** The manufacturer states the unit does not need to be bolted to the floor. [Manufacturer page](https://www.roguefitness.com/rogue-hr-2-half-rack).
- **REP Apollo Half Rack.** Flat-foot base; the manufacturer states it eliminates the need to bolt down. [Manufacturer page](https://repfitness.com/products/apollo-rack-builder).
- **Titan T-3 Short Squat Stand.** The manufacturer states no floor bolting is required. [Manufacturer page](https://titan.fitness/products/t-3-series-short-squat-stand).

## Buy this / skip that

**Buy the Titan T-2 if** you have a ceiling under 84 inches and want a full cage with pin-and-pipe safeties and a pull-up bar already included. It is the only renter-friendly compact power rack in this catalog that clears 7 feet. Know that Titan recommends bolting it down for added stability even though bolting is not required, and that overall width, depth, and footprint are Unverified beyond the unlabeled 57 in x 50 in figure.

**Buy the Titan T-3 Short Squat Stand if** you need a short, unbolted stand for a low room and can live without a pull-up bar and without included safeties. Spotter arms are sold separately. Base width and depth stay Unverified beyond the unlabeled 47 in x 48 in figure.

**Buy the REP Apollo (80" uprights) if** you want a 3x3 half rack that still fits under 84 inches (4 in of clearance) and a flat-foot base the manufacturer says does not need to be bolted down. Confirm pull-up bar, J-cups, and safeties in the builder, because those inclusions are Unverified at the catalog level. Do not pick the 93-inch upright option for a 7-foot room.

**Buy the REP PR-1100 if** you have more than 85 inches of ceiling (11 in of clearance under 96 in), want a full cage with included pin-and-pipe safeties and J-cups, and need a rack that cannot be bolted down — which is also the limitation, because you have no bolt-down fallback. Skip it for 7-foot rooms. The 700 lb capacity and 14-gauge 2x2 uprights are the lightest-duty published cage in this set.

**Buy the Rogue HR-2 if** you have close to 8-foot ceilings, want a 3x3 half rack that the manufacturer says does not need to be bolted, and prefer the smallest published footprint in this group (16.33 sq ft). Skip it for 7-foot rooms. Plan on buying safeties separately, and treat hole size, capacity, and unit weight as unpublished (weight is only a 225–255 lb range).

**Skip the squat stand** if you need an included pull-up bar or included safeties. **Skip the PR-1100 and HR-2** if the ceiling is under 84 inches. **Skip the T-2** if you cannot bolt the floor and are not comfortable ignoring Titan's recommendation to bolt it for stability.
