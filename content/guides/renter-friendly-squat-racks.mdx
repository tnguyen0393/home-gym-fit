## Quick verdict

These racks are freestanding: wall mounting is not required, and floor bolting is not required. That does not mean every one is stable without a bolt, or that all five fit a 7-foot ceiling.

If the room is under 84 inches, only three of these five clear that height.

- **Best full cage under 7 ft:** The [Titan T-2](/products/titan-t-2-series-power-rack-71) is 71 in tall and includes J-cups, a pull-up bar, and pin-and-pipe safeties. Titan recommends bolting it down for added stability.
- **Shortest stand:** The [Titan T-3 Short Squat Stand](/products/titan-t-3-series-short-squat-stand) is 73.5 in tall. No pull-up bar and no safeties are included.
- **3x3 half rack under 7 ft:** The [REP Apollo (80" Uprights)](/products/rep-apollo-half-rack-80) is 80 in tall, leaving a tight 4 in under 84 in. Pull-up bar, J-cups, and safeties depend on builder selections.
- **Full cage for 8 ft rooms:** The [REP PR-1100](/products/rep-pr-1100-power-rack) is 85 in, too tall for 84 in, with included safeties. It cannot be bolted down.
- **Smallest published footprint:** The [Rogue HR-2 (90" Front Uprights)](/products/rogue-hr-2-half-rack-90) is 92.25 in, too tall for 84 in. The manufacturer says it does not need to be bolted, and safeties are not included.

Minimum ceiling is not published on all five. Clearance figures are ceiling height minus verified rack height, not a manufacturer-stated minimum.

The PR-1100's multi-grip pull-up bar can be inverted to reduce height, but the reduced height is not published. This guide still treats the rack as 85 inches.

## Full cage vs half rack vs squat stand

- **Full cages:** The [REP PR-1100](/products/rep-pr-1100-power-rack) and [Titan T-2](/products/titan-t-2-series-power-rack-71) both include J-cups, a pull-up bar, and pin-and-pipe safeties. Both use 2x2, 14-gauge uprights.
- **T-2 extras:** 850 lb rackable and 2,200 lb whole-rack capacity; 1.25 in pull-up bar at a 69 in max height; two weight-plate horns.
- **T-2 hole size:** Listed as 1 in and also as 26 mm.
- **PR-1100 watch out:** 700 lb capacity and 3/8 in hardware. Muscle-ups and banded movements are not recommended; there are no band-peg holes.
- **Half racks:** The [Rogue HR-2](/products/rogue-hr-2-half-rack-90) and [REP Apollo](/products/rep-apollo-half-rack-80) are both 3x3, 11-gauge, and freestanding.
- **HR-2 extras:** Includes a Monster Lite J-cup pair and a 43 in single skinny pull-up bar. A 16 in pin/pipe safety set is sold separately.
- **HR-2 base:** 2x3 in, 11-gauge. Hole size and capacity are not published.
- **Apollo extras:** 1,000 lb rackable capacity and a flat-foot base; 5000 Series attachments, not Ares or Belt Squat compatible. Inclusions depend on builder selections.
- **Squat stand:** The [Titan T-3 Short Squat Stand](/products/titan-t-3-series-short-squat-stand) has 2x3, 11-gauge uprights, 0.6875 in holes, and a 1,000 lb capacity.
- **Squat stand inclusions:** J-cups are included; the pull-up bar and safeties are not. Spotter arms are sold separately.

## Freestanding stability and no-bolting notes

All five can be used without wall mounting, and floor bolting is not required. The manufacturers still do not say the same thing about leaving them unbolted.

- **REP PR-1100:** The manufacturer states the PR-1100 cannot be bolted down. There is no bolt-down path if the rack shifts. [Manufacturer page](https://repfitness.com/products/pr-1100-power-rack)
- **Titan T-2:** Rubber feet with bolt-down options. Titan recommends bolting down for added stability, even though bolting is not required. [Manufacturer page](https://titan.fitness/products/t2-series-power-rack)
- **Rogue HR-2:** The manufacturer states the unit does not need to be bolted to the floor. [Manufacturer page](https://www.roguefitness.com/rogue-hr-2-half-rack)
- **REP Apollo:** Flat-foot base; the manufacturer states it eliminates the need to bolt down. [Manufacturer page](https://repfitness.com/products/apollo-rack-builder)
- **Titan T-3 Short Squat Stand:** The manufacturer states no floor bolting is required. [Manufacturer page](https://titan.fitness/products/t-3-series-short-squat-stand)
