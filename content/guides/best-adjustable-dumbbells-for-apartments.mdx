## Quick verdict

All five of these adjustable dumbbells are sold as pairs. None of the manufacturers publish a storage footprint in square feet.

Do not interpolate a footprint from tray size or dumbbell length. Use the published trays, max lengths, and drop statements instead.

Two include measured trays. The [REP QuickDraw](/products/rep-quickdraw-adjustable-dumbbell-60lb) cradle is 20.6 × 8.45 × 4.7 in. The [Snode AD80](/products/snode-ad80-adjustable-dumbbell) cradle is 19.31 × 7.77 × 3.98 in.

The [REP x PÉPIN FAST](/products/rep-x-pepin-fast-series-adjustable-dumbbell-125lb) includes a tray without published dimensions. Tray inclusion is not published for the [PowerBlock Pro 100 EXP](/products/powerblock-pro-100-exp-stage-1-40lb). The [Ironmaster Quick-Lock](/products/ironmaster-quick-lock-adjustable-dumbbell-75lb) has no tray.

- **Shortest published length:** The [Ironmaster Quick-Lock](/products/ironmaster-quick-lock-adjustable-dumbbell-75lb) is 14.5 in long at 75 lb, and 9 in long at 20 lb. No tray is included.
- **Highest top weight:** The [REP x PÉPIN FAST](/products/rep-x-pepin-fast-series-adjustable-dumbbell-125lb) goes from 10–125 lb in 5 lb steps. The 18.3 in length is published for the 125 lb kit.
- **Measured tray, lock-switch:** The [REP QuickDraw](/products/rep-quickdraw-adjustable-dumbbell-60lb) is 5–60 lb with a 20.6 × 8.45 × 4.7 in cradle. Occasional drops are tolerated; repeated drops are not covered by warranty.
- **Expandable selector-pin starter:** The [PowerBlock Pro 100 EXP](/products/powerblock-pro-100-exp-stage-1-40lb) Stage 1 is 5–40 lb and expands to 60, 80, or 100 lb. Height, width, and length are not published for this stage.
- **Dial adjuster with a drop claim:** The [Snode AD80](/products/snode-ad80-adjustable-dumbbell) is 10–80 lb in 10 lb steps. The manufacturer states it is drop-proof up to 32 in at multiple angles.

## Apartment fit

The QuickDraw cradle is 7.9 in tall with the dumbbell in place, and the empty cradle weighs 4.64 lb.

The Snode AD80 all-iron cradle weighs 15.3 lb.

The PÉPIN includes all-metal cradles with no published dimensions, and is designed for flat storage only. PowerBlock does not state whether a tray is included. Ironmaster includes no tray.

None of the five publish a storage footprint in square feet. A PÉPIN Tech Specs footprint figure has no stated basis, so it is left unpublished.

PowerBlock states 1.75 feet of space, which is a linear figure, not a square-foot footprint. Stands are not included with any of these five kits.

Adjustment style and drop language vary. Two of the five pages publish no drop rating.

- **Lock-switch (QuickDraw):** Lock-N-Load switches move in 10 lb steps, plus two 2.5 lb micro plates for a 5 lb increment. Occasional drops are tolerated; repeated drops are not covered by warranty.
- **Selector-pin (PÉPIN and PowerBlock):** The PÉPIN uses magnetic pop-pins in 10 lb steps, plus 2.5 lb micro plates for a 5 lb increment. PowerBlock uses a magnetic selector pin.
- **Dial (Snode AD80):** A single cradle dial. The manufacturer states drop-proof up to 32 in at multiple angles, from a 1,000-drop test.
- **Quick-lock screw (Ironmaster):** Locking screws are slower. The manufacturer states: "Heavy duty, go ahead and drop them."

## Fit verdicts

**[REP QuickDraw](/products/rep-quickdraw-adjustable-dumbbell-60lb)**
- **Best for:** A lock-switch 5–60 lb pair with a published 20.6 × 8.45 × 4.7 in cradle and a 32 mm handle.
- **Watch out:** Max length is 18.5 in at 60 lb, and this pair is not expandable. Repeated drops are not covered by warranty.
- [Manufacturer page](https://repfitness.com/products/quickdraw-adjustable-dumbbell-lb)

**[REP x PÉPIN FAST](/products/rep-x-pepin-fast-series-adjustable-dumbbell-125lb)**
- **Best for:** The highest top weight here, 10–125 lb in 5 lb steps, with a 34 mm handle and a selector-pin change.
- **Watch out:** The 18.3 in length is for the 125 lb kit. Tray size, footprint, and a drop rating are not published. It is designed for flat storage only.
- **Also note:** Original PÉPIN components are not compatible. A stand and cart are sold separately.
- [Manufacturer page](https://repfitness.com/products/rep-x-pepin-fast-series-adjustable-dumbbell)

**[PowerBlock Pro 100 EXP](/products/powerblock-pro-100-exp-stage-1-40lb)**
- **Best for:** A 5–40 lb Stage 1 pair that expands to 60, 80, or 100 lb, with a 38 mm knurled handle and a 4.88 in grip.
- **Watch out:** Increments are a non-uniform 2.5 or 5 lb. Size, tray inclusion, footprint, and a drop rating are not published.
- [Manufacturer page](https://www.powerblock.com/products/pro-100-exp-adjustable-dumbbells)

**[Ironmaster Quick-Lock](/products/ironmaster-quick-lock-adjustable-dumbbell-75lb)**

**[Snode AD80](/products/snode-ad80-adjustable-dumbbell)**
- **Best for:** A dial-adjusted 10–80 lb pair with a published 19.31 × 7.77 × 3.98 in iron cradle and a drop-proof claim up to 32 in.
- **Watch out:** Increments are 10 lb, larger than the 2.5 or 5 lb steps on the others. Expandability is not published.
- [Manufacturer page](https://snodesport.com/products/ad-80-quick-adjusting-dumbbells)
