## Quick verdict

All four folding wall-mount racks are shorter than 96 inches when open. That is not the same as a clean 8-foot fit.

Fold direction is the real split: inward-fold racks do not spend extra ceiling height when stored.

The PRx folds upward and is the only rack here with a published minimum ceiling.

- **Most headroom under 8 ft:** The [Titan T-3 Folding Power Rack](/products/titan-t-3-series-folding-power-rack-82-21-5) is the shortest and narrowest, with a 5 in folded depth. Fold direction is not published, and wall-mount hardware is not included.
- **Smallest published footprint:** The [REP PR-4100](/products/rep-pr-4100-folding-wall-mount-rack-21-5) folds inward to a published 6 in. Clearance under 96 in is the tightest of the four.
- **More overhead than the REP:** The [Rogue RML-3W](/products/rogue-rml-3w-fold-back-wall-mount-rack-21-5) leaves more clearance under 96 in, and the 21.5 in version can fold inward or outward. Folded depth is not published.
- **Thinnest stored profile:** The [PRx Profile PRO](/products/prx-profile-pro-folding-squat-rack-89) folds upward to 4 in. A 96 in ceiling is a modified install, not a standard one.

Minimum ceiling is not published for the REP, Rogue, and Titan. Clearance below is 96 in minus each rack's verified height.

| Rack | Verified height | Min ceiling | Clearance under 96 in | Fold direction |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Titan T-3 Folding](/products/titan-t-3-series-folding-power-rack-82-21-5) | 82.375 in | Not published | 13.625 in | Not published |
| [PRx Profile PRO](/products/prx-profile-pro-folding-squat-rack-89) | 89 in | 94 in / 107 in | 7 in | Upward |
| [Rogue RML-3W](/products/rogue-rml-3w-fold-back-wall-mount-rack-21-5) | 90.375 in | Not published | 5.625 in | Inward |
| [REP PR-4100](/products/rep-pr-4100-folding-wall-mount-rack-21-5) | 92.3 in | Not published | 3.7 in | Inward |

- **PRx ceiling:** 94 in is the modified-install minimum; 107 in is recommended for a standard install.
- **PRx 7 in figure:** That is space above the 89 in uprights when the rack is down, not fold-up clearance.
- **PRx vs 96 in:** A 96 in ceiling is 2 in above the modified minimum and 11 in short of the 107 in standard recommendation. Folded-up height is not published.
- **Rogue fold:** The 21.5 in version can also fold outward.
- **Pull-up height:** Only Titan publishes a max pull-up bar height, 80 in. Do not treat leftover ceiling as pull-up clearance.

## Mounting, stringers, and studs

All four racks require wall mounting, and none is renter-friendly.

Floor-bolt requirements are not published, and stud spacing is not published for the REP, Rogue, and Titan.

**[REP PR-4100](/products/rep-pr-4100-folding-wall-mount-rack-21-5)**
- **Watch out:** A wood stringer of two 2x10x56 in boards is not included. The REP Stringer is not compatible.
- **Specs that matter:** Concrete mounting uses included M10x90 expansion bolts. Capacity is 1,000 lb when properly mounted.
- [Manufacturer page](https://repfitness.com/products/pr-4100-folding-wall-mount-squat-power-rack)

**[Rogue RML-3W](/products/rogue-rml-3w-fold-back-wall-mount-rack-21-5)**
- **Watch out:** An optional stringer pair is sold separately, highly recommended, and requires 4 wood studs.
- **Specs that matter:** Hardware is 5/8 in. If you use spotter arms, Rogue says a contractor should check the wall attachment.
- [Manufacturer page](https://www.roguefitness.com/rogue-rml-3w-fold-back-wall-mount-rack)

**[Titan T-3 Folding Power Rack](/products/titan-t-3-series-folding-power-rack-82-21-5)**
- **Watch out:** Wall mounting hardware is not included.
- [Manufacturer page](https://titan.fitness/products/t3-series-folding-power-rack)

**[PRx Profile PRO](/products/prx-profile-pro-folding-squat-rack-89)**
- **Best for:** Wood studs at 16 in or 24 in on-center, with all wood-stud brackets and hardware included.
- **Watch out:** A ledger board is required if the studs are unevenly spaced. The fold-up is hydraulic-shock assisted.
- [Manufacturer page](https://prxperformance.com/products/profile-pro-bar)
