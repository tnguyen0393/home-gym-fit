## Fit verdict for a 96-inch ceiling

All four folding wall-mount racks in this catalog are shorter than 96 inches when the rack is in the open (in-use) position. That is not the same as a clean 8-foot fit.

The real split is fold direction. The [REP PR-4100](/products/rep-pr-4100-folding-wall-mount-rack-21-5) and [Rogue RML-3W](/products/rogue-rml-3w-fold-back-wall-mount-rack-21-5) fold inward, so stored height is not the ceiling problem. The [PRx Profile PRO](/products/prx-profile-pro-folding-squat-rack-89) folds upward, and PRx is the only rack here with a published minimum ceiling: 94 inches for a modified install, 107 inches recommended for a standard install. A 96-inch ceiling clears that modified minimum by 2 inches and sits 11 inches short of the standard-install recommendation.

The [Titan T-3 Series Folding Power Rack](/products/titan-t-3-series-folding-power-rack-82-21-5) is the height outlier. At 82.375 inches, it leaves 13.625 inches of clearance under 96 inches, the most of the four. Fold direction for the Titan is Not published.

Manufacturer minimum ceiling is Unverified for the REP, Rogue, and Titan. The clearance figures below are 96 inches minus each rack's verified height.

| [Rogue RML-3W](/products/rogue-rml-3w-fold-back-wall-mount-rack-21-5) | 90.375 in | Not published | 5.625 in | inward (21.5 in version can also fold outward) |
| [REP PR-4100](/products/rep-pr-4100-folding-wall-mount-rack-21-5) | 92.3 in | Not published | 3.7 in | inward |

The REP PR-4100 is the tightest inward-folding option under 8-foot ceilings: 3.7 inches between the top of a 92.3-inch rack and the ceiling. The Rogue is 1.925 inches shorter than the REP and leaves 5.625 inches. Neither figure tells you pull-up bar clearance to the ceiling; only Titan publishes a max pull-up bar height, 80 inches.

On the PRx, do not treat the 7-inch figure as fold-up clearance. That rack stores by swinging upward. PRx has not published a separate folded-up height, and it has not published a depth figure for a 96-inch ceiling. What is published: standard install at a 107-inch ceiling uses 21.75 inches of rack depth; a modified install at a 94-inch ceiling reduces depth to 16 inches. A 96-inch room falls between those two published points. Do not interpolate a depth.

The Titan is the narrowest on the wall at 46 inches. The REP is 48 inches wide with a 7.17 sq ft footprint, the smallest published floor imprint of the four. Folded depth is a different story. The PRx is the thinnest stored profile at 4 inches, because it folds up, not in. The Titan folds to 5 inches. The REP folds to 6 inches. Rogue folded depth is Unverified; the manufacturer only states "less than 5 inches."

Use the current REP spec-table values above (92.3 in height, 48 in width, 6 in folded depth). The same product record's legacy description lists 92 in, 48.5 in, and 6.5 in. Those conflict with the spec table and are not used here.

## Mounting, stringers, and studs

All four racks require wall mounting. None is renter-friendly. Floor-bolt requirements are Unverified for every rack in this set. Stud spacing is Unverified for the REP, Rogue, and Titan.

**REP PR-4100.** A wood stringer of two 2x10x56-inch boards is not included. The REP Stringer is not compatible. Concrete mounting uses included M10x90 expansion bolts. Capacity is 1,000 lb "when properly mounted." [Manufacturer page](https://repfitness.com/products/pr-4100-folding-wall-mount-squat-power-rack).

**Rogue RML-3W.** An optional stringer pair is sold separately and highly recommended; it requires 4 wood studs. Hardware is 5/8 inch. Rogue states that wall attachment should be checked by a contractor if you use spotter arms. [Manufacturer page](https://www.roguefitness.com/rogue-rml-3w-fold-back-wall-mount-rack).

**Titan T-3 Folding Power Rack.** Wall mounting hardware is not included. [Manufacturer page](https://titan.fitness/products/t3-series-folding-power-rack).

**PRx Profile PRO.** Wall brackets mount to 16-inch or 24-inch on-center studs. A ledger board is required if the studs are unevenly spaced. All wall mounting brackets and hardware for wood-stud installation are included. The fold-up is hydraulic-shock assisted. [Manufacturer page](https://prxperformance.com/products/profile-pro-bar).

## Buy this / skip that

**Buy the Titan T-3 Folding Power Rack if** you have an 8-foot ceiling and want the most published headroom (13.625 in), the narrowest wall width (46 in), and a 5-inch folded depth. Know that wall mounting hardware is not included, and fold direction is Unverified.

**Buy the Rogue RML-3W if** you want a 3x3 inward-folding rack with more overhead clearance than the REP (5.625 in vs 3.7 in) and the option to fold the 21.5-inch version inward or outward. You will give up wall width and open depth, folded depth stays Unverified, and the recommended stringer is sold separately.

**Buy the REP PR-4100 if** you need the smallest published footprint (7.17 sq ft), a 48-inch wall width, a fixed 21.5-inch open depth, and a published 6-inch folded depth, and you can live with only 3.7 inches of ceiling clearance. Plan on providing the 2x10x56-inch wood stringer; the REP Stringer will not work.

**Buy the PRx Profile PRO if** you specifically want a 4-inch upward-fold profile, wood studs at 16-inch or 24-inch centers, and included wood-stud mounting hardware, and you accept a modified install under an 8-foot ceiling. A 96-inch ceiling is legal by PRx's 94-inch modified minimum, but it is 11 inches short of the 107-inch standard-install recommendation, and open depth at 96 inches is not published (16 in at 94 in; 21.75 in at 107 in).

**Skip the PRx under an 8-foot ceiling if** you need the standard 21.75-inch depth or you cannot accept an upward fold that spends ceiling space. **Skip the REP** if 3.7 inches of overhead clearance is too tight for pull-ups. **Skip the Rogue** if a 56.1875-inch on-wall width or 24.75-inch open depth will not fit the wall you have.
